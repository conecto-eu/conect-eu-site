Frequently asked questions

How can I report a listing?

Open a ride, parcel, service, or job listing posted by someone else. Tap Report this listing to open an email draft with the listing reference. Add the reason for your report and send the email.

If the email app cannot open, Conecto displays the support address and listing reference. Include that reference and a description of the issue in an email to support.

How can I block or unblock a user?

Open one of their listings, tap Block this user, and confirm. This hides their listings for you on the device you are using. To unblock them, go to Profile → Blocked users.

How can I delete my account?

Sign in to Conecto. Open Profile, scroll to the Account section, and tap Delete Account. Follow the prompts to confirm.

To stop future subscription renewals, cancel your subscription separately in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. If you cannot sign in, see the account deletion instructions.

How can I send feedback or report a technical issue?

Go to Profile → Help & feedback. Choose Send feedback for suggestions or Report a problem for a technical issue. You can also email our support team directly.
