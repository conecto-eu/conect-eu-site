Frequently Asked Questions

How can I report a listing?

Open the details of a ride, parcel, service, or job listing posted by someone else. Tap Report this listing to open an email draft with the listing reference. Add the reason for your report and send the email.

If your email app cannot open, Conecto displays our support address and the listing reference. Copy the reference into an email to support and tell us what happened.

How can I block or unblock a user?

Open one of their listings, tap Block this user, and confirm. This hides their listings for you on the device you are using. To unblock them, go to Profile → Blocked users.

How can I delete my account?

Sign in to Conecto. Open Profile, scroll to the Account section, and tap Delete Account. Follow the prompts to confirm.

If you have an active subscription, cancel it separately in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings to stop future renewals. For help if you cannot sign in, see the account deletion instructions.

How can I send feedback or report a technical issue?

In Conecto, go to Profile → Help & feedback. Choose Send feedback for suggestions or Report a problem for a technical issue. You can also email our support team directly.
