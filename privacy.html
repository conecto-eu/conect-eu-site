What Conecto Does

Conecto is a mobile app that helps users create, browse, and manage listings in several categories, including rides, parcels, services, and jobs.

Users can search for listings, save favorites, contact listing owners through the contact details provided in listings, and manage their own listings.

Some features require a free account. Optional paid subscription plans, such as Conecto Plus and Conecto Pro, and optional listing promotion features may be available through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

Information We Collect

We collect information needed to provide, secure, maintain, and improve the app.

The categories below describe the information we may collect, depending on how you use Conecto.

Account and Authentication Information

When you create or use an account, we may collect or receive:

  • your email address;
  • your name or display name, if provided;
  • your Firebase user ID or other internal account identifiers;
  • your authentication provider, such as email/password, Google, or Apple;
  • account creation and last login timestamps;
  • account preferences, such as language and color scheme.

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the name and email address made available by Google or Apple, depending on your account settings and the information shared by those providers.

Conecto does not directly store your password. Password-based authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication.

Listing and User Content

When you create or manage a listing, we collect the information you choose to enter. Depending on the listing type, this may include:

Rides

  • origin, destination, intermediate stops, and route details;
  • dates, times, trip type, recurring days, or specific dates;
  • number of seats, luggage size, vehicle type, amenities, and trailer availability;
  • prices, currency, price policy, and cancellation policy;
  • driver name, company name, contact details, and free-text notes.

Parcels

  • origin, destination, intermediate cities, and route details;
  • dates, times, trip type, recurring days, or specific dates;
  • vehicle type and transport options, such as trailer, refrigerated transport, or car transport;
  • base prices, price tiers, currency, and negotiability;
  • driver name, company name, contact details, and free-text notes.

Services

  • country, city, area, address, map links, and service location details;
  • service category, service name, description, menu or website links, and business hours;
  • service features, such as mobile service, towing, delivery, takeaway, cuisine, or price level;
  • phone number, email address, WhatsApp, website, and social/profile links.

Jobs

  • country, city, area, job title, category, and listing mode;
  • description, collaboration type, salary information, requirements, and experience level;
  • work-related details such as accommodation, transport, desired work type, driver license, car availability, or immediate availability;
  • contact name, company name, phone number, WhatsApp number, and email address.

Public Listing and Contact Information

When you publish a listing, the listing content may be visible to other users and, where public listing pages or public indexes are available, to visitors who are not signed in.

You may choose to include public contact information in your listings, such as:

  • name, contact name, driver name, or company name;
  • phone number or WhatsApp number;
  • email address;
  • website, menu, map, social media, profile, or similar public links;
  • other public contact details you choose to publish.

This information may be visible as part of your public listing. Please include only information you are comfortable making public.

Favorites, Preferences, and Local Data

Conecto may store your saved listings, favorites, or bookmarks so that you can access them again later. Favorites are app data associated with your account and are not displayed as public listing content.

We may also store app preferences, such as:

  • preferred language;
  • preferred display theme;
  • preferred currency;
  • haptic feedback setting;
  • notification preference;
  • list filter preferences.

Some preferences may be stored locally on your device. Some account-related preferences may be synced to your account.

Subscription and Purchase Information

Conecto may offer auto-renewable subscription plans, such as Conecto Plus and Conecto Pro, and optional one-time purchases, such as listing promotion features.

Conecto does not directly process or store your payment card details.

Payments are processed by:

  • Apple through the Apple App Store on iOS;
  • Google through Google Play on Android.

Conecto uses RevenueCat to help manage subscriptions, purchases, promotion entitlement, billing status, purchase restoration, and subscription access.

We may receive and store subscription-related or purchase-related information such as:

  • subscription plan, entitlement, and access status;
  • product identifier, offering identifier, package identifier, or promotion product identifier;
  • purchase identifier, transaction-related identifiers, or purchase date where available;
  • expiration date, trial status, renewal status, cancellation state, or billing issue state;
  • store type, such as App Store or Google Play;
  • RevenueCat App User ID and related subscription management identifiers;
  • backend access mirror data used to decide which paid features are available.

We use this information to determine which paid features you can access, verify subscriptions and purchases, manage listing promotions, restore purchases, and display your subscription status in the app.

We do not receive your full payment card number or full billing details from Apple, Google, or RevenueCat.

Notifications

Conecto uses local notifications.

Local notifications are generated and scheduled on your device. We do not use remote push notifications unless this changes in a future version.

Conecto may schedule notifications such as:

  • reminders to check new listings;
  • reminders for eligible listings that are close to expiring;
  • reminders for eligible listing promotions that are close to ending.

You can disable notifications from the Profile screen or from your device settings.

Device, Diagnostics, and Technical Information

We use Firebase Crashlytics to help identify and fix crashes, non-fatal errors, and stability issues.

Crashlytics may collect technical or diagnostic information such as:

  • crash reports, non-fatal error reports, exception messages, and stack traces;
  • device, operating system, app version, and technical state related to the error;
  • screen, operation, or app area where the error occurred;
  • listing type, entity ID, counts, flags, or other non-content diagnostic metadata where applicable;
  • authentication state and Firebase user ID as an internal account identifier;
  • high-level billing diagnostic breadcrumbs, such as purchase or restore status, used for debugging billing issues.

Crashlytics diagnostics may be linked to your user or account identifier so we can diagnose and fix problems affecting your account.

We do not intentionally log names, email addresses, phone numbers, free-text listing content, addresses, or GPS data in crash reports.

Conecto does not use analytics SDKs, advertising networks, cookies, IDFA, App Tracking Transparency tracking, cross-app tracking technologies, or advertising tracking domains. The hosted legal page is static and does not set cookies.

Location Information

Conecto does not request access to your device's GPS, precise location, approximate location, or real-time location sensor.

The app does not request device location permission and does not perform background location tracking.

Location-related information in Conecto is based on information users enter or select when creating listings, such as city names, country names, origin, destination, intermediate stops, service areas, job areas, addresses, route information, or map links.

Some city, route, service, or place records may include reference coordinates used to support listing and route functionality. These coordinates are associated with entered or selected places and are not collected from your device's GPS sensor.

Data Retention

We keep account information and listing data for as long as your account is active or as long as needed to provide the app.

Listings that you delete, deactivate, or that expire may be removed from public display or public indexes where applicable.

When you delete your account, we delete app-owned account-related data as described in the Account Deletion section below, subject to technical, legal, security, and provider retention limits.

We may retain certain limited records after account deletion where necessary for:

  • legal compliance;
  • security;
  • fraud prevention;
  • payment or transaction integrity;
  • audit and integrity verification;
  • resolving disputes;
  • enforcing our terms.

For example, we may retain a technical deletion record containing limited metadata such as user ID, deletion status, deletion timestamps, and deletion counts. This record is retained for integrity and audit purposes.

Apple, Google, RevenueCat, Firebase, Crashlytics, and similar providers may retain historical records according to their own policies and legal obligations.

Account Deletion and Data Deletion

You can delete your Conecto account directly from within the app:

Profile → Delete Account

If you cannot access the app, you can also find account deletion instructions and contact details on our public account deletion page: https://conect-eu-app.web.app/delete-account.html

Depending on your sign-in method, you may be asked to re-authenticate before deletion.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

When you delete your account, Conecto is designed to remove app-owned data where applicable, including by:

  1. canceling scheduled local notifications;
  2. deleting public listing index entries across listing categories, such as rides, parcels, services, and jobs;
  3. deleting documents in your user account collection, including profile data, listings, favorites, and related app data;
  4. deleting or attempting to delete your Firebase Authentication account;
  5. clearing user-scoped local Realm data, such as your local listings, favorites, sync state, and billing cache;
  6. clearing local on-device preferences where applicable;
  7. signing you out locally and logging out of RevenueCat on the device where applicable.

Some static or shared local app data, such as cached place records, may remain because it is not specific to your account.

Some limited records may be retained as described in the Data Retention section above.

Historical records may also be retained by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, Firebase, Crashlytics, or similar providers according to their own policies and legal obligations.

Important Note About App Store and Google Play Subscriptions

Deleting your Conecto account does not automatically cancel an active subscription purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

If you have an active Conecto Plus or Conecto Pro subscription, you must manage or cancel it separately through your store subscription settings.

iOS

App Store → Apple ID → Subscriptions

Android

Google Play Store → Account → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions

Your paid access may remain active until the end of the current billing period. If you do not cancel your subscription through the appropriate store, it may continue to renew and be charged by Apple or Google.
