# Privacy Policy > **Last updated:** June 9, 2026 --- ## 1. Who We Are Conecto is operated by its developer, referred to in this Privacy Policy as "we," "us," or "our." If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, you can contact us at: 📧 **Email:** [conecto.eu@gmail.com](mailto:conecto.eu@gmail.com) --- ## 2. What Conecto Does Conecto is a mobile app that helps users create, browse, and manage listings in several categories, including rides, parcels, services, and jobs. Users can search for listings, save favorites, contact listing owners through the contact details provided in listings, and manage their own listings. Some features require a free account. Optional paid subscription plans, such as Conecto Plus and Conecto Pro, and optional listing promotion features may be available through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. --- ## 3. Information We Collect We collect information needed to provide, secure, maintain, and improve the app. The categories below describe the information we may collect, depending on how you use Conecto. --- ## 4. Account and Authentication Information When you create an account, we may collect: - your email address; - your display name, if provided; - your Firebase user ID; - your authentication provider, such as email/password, Google, or Apple; - account creation and last login timestamps; - account preferences, such as language and color scheme. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the name and email address made available by Google or Apple, depending on your account settings and the information shared by those providers. Conecto does not directly store your password. Password-based authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication. --- ## 5. Listing and User Content When you create or manage a listing, we collect the information you choose to enter. Depending on the listing type, this may include: ### 🚗 Rides - origin city; - destination city; - intermediate stops; - dates and times; - trip type; - number of seats; - luggage size; - vehicle type; - amenities; - price; - price policy; - cancellation policy; - free-text notes. ### 📦 Parcels - origin city; - destination city; - intermediate cities; - dates and times; - trip type; - price tiers; - vehicle type; - transport options; - free-text notes. ### 🛠️ Services - city; - area; - country; - service category; - description; - business hours; - social links; - website links; - other service-related details you choose to provide. ### 💼 Jobs - city; - country; - job title; - category; - collaboration type; - description; - salary information; - requirements; - experience level; - other work-related details you choose to provide. --- ## 6. Contact Information in Listings You may choose to include contact information in your listings, such as: - phone number; - WhatsApp number; - email address; - contact name; - driver name; - company name. This information may be visible to other users as part of your public listing. You are responsible for deciding what contact information you include in a listing. --- ## 7. Favorites, Preferences, and Local Data Conecto may store your saved listings or favorites so that you can access them again later. We may also store app preferences, such as: - preferred language; - preferred display theme; - preferred currency; - haptic feedback setting; - notification preference; - list filter preferences. Some preferences may be stored locally on your device. Some account-related preferences may be synced to your account. --- ## 8. Subscription and Purchase Information Conecto may offer auto-renewable subscription plans, such as Conecto Plus and Conecto Pro, and optional one-time purchases, such as listing promotion features. Conecto does not directly process or store your payment card details. Payments are processed by: - Apple through the Apple App Store on iOS; - Google through Google Play on Android. Conecto uses RevenueCat to help manage subscription and purchase status. We may receive and store subscription-related information such as: - subscription plan; - subscription status; - product identifier; - offering or package identifier; - expiration date; - trial status, if applicable; - renewal status; - store type, such as App Store or Google Play; - RevenueCat App User ID. We use this information to determine which paid features you can access and to display your subscription status in the app. We do not receive your full payment card number or full billing details from Apple, Google, or RevenueCat. --- ## 9. Notifications Conecto uses local notifications. Local notifications are generated and scheduled on your device. We do not use remote push notifications unless this changes in a future version. Conecto may schedule notifications such as: - reminders to check new listings; - reminders that one of your listings is close to expiring; - reminders that a listing promotion is close to ending. You can disable notifications from the Profile screen or from your device settings. --- ## 10. Device, Diagnostics, and Technical Information We use Firebase Crashlytics to help identify and fix crashes and errors. Crashlytics may collect technical information such as: - crash reports; - error messages; - stack traces; - app state related to the error; - operation or screen where the error occurred; - listing type involved in the error, where applicable; - authentication state; - Firebase user ID as an internal identifier. We do not intentionally log names, email addresses, phone numbers, listing text, or free-text user content in crash reports. We do not use third-party analytics SDKs for advertising tracking. --- ## 11. Location Information Conecto does not request access to your device's GPS or real-time location sensor. The app does not request device location permission. Location-related information in Conecto is based on information users enter or select when creating listings, such as city names, country names, origin, destination, intermediate stops, and route information. Some city or place records may include reference coordinates used to support listing and route functionality. These coordinates are associated with places selected in the app and are not collected from your device's GPS sensor. --- ## 12. How We Use Information We use information to: - create and manage your account; - authenticate you securely; - publish, display, and manage listings; - allow users to contact listing owners through listing contact details; - save and sync favorites; - store preferences; - provide subscription-based features; - verify subscription and purchase status; - restore purchases; - schedule local notifications; - diagnose crashes and technical issues; - improve app reliability and security; - prevent abuse, fraud, or misuse; - respond to support or privacy requests. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your personal information for third-party advertising. --- ## 13. Legal Basis / Reasons for Processing Depending on applicable law, we process information for the following reasons: - to provide the app and its features; - to perform our agreement with you; - to manage your account and listings; - to provide subscription and purchase-related functionality; - to maintain app security and prevent abuse; - to diagnose and fix technical issues; - to comply with legal obligations where applicable; - based on our legitimate interest in operating, securing, and improving the app. --- ## 14. Sharing Information with Service Providers We may share information with service providers that help us operate Conecto. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual terms with us. We do not sell your personal information. --- ## 15. Third-Party Services Conecto may use the following third-party services: ### Firebase Authentication Used for account creation and sign-in. Google processes payments according to Google's own terms and privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party services. --- ## 16. Data Retention We keep account information and listing data for as long as your account is active or as long as needed to provide the app. Listings that you delete or deactivate may be removed from public display. When you delete your account, we delete account-related data as described in the Account Deletion section below. We may retain certain limited records after account deletion where necessary for: - legal compliance; - security; - fraud prevention; - payment or transaction integrity; - audit and integrity verification; - resolving disputes; - enforcing our terms. For example, we may retain a technical deletion record containing limited metadata such as user ID, deletion status, deletion timestamps, and deletion counts. This record is retained for integrity and audit purposes. Firebase, RevenueCat, Apple, and Google may retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations. --- ## 17. Account Deletion and Data Deletion You can delete your Conecto account directly from within the app: > **Profile → Delete Account** Depending on your sign-in method, you may be asked to re-authenticate before deletion. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. When you delete your account, Conecto is designed to: 1. cancel scheduled local notifications; 2. delete your public listing index entries across listing categories, such as rides, parcels, services, and jobs; 3. delete documents in your user account collection, including profile data, listings, and favorites; 4. delete your Firebase Authentication account; 5. clear your local Realm database; 6. clear local on-device preferences where applicable. Some limited records may be retained as described in the Data Retention section above. Transaction records may also be retained by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat according to their own policies. --- ## 18. Important Note About App Store and Google Play Subscriptions Deleting your Conecto account does not automatically cancel an active subscription purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. If you have an active Conecto Plus or Conecto Pro subscription, you must manage or cancel it separately through your store subscription settings. **On iOS:** > App Store → Apple ID → Subscriptions **On Android:** > Google Play Store → Account → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions Your paid access may remain active until the end of the current billing period. If you do not cancel your subscription through the appropriate store, it may continue to renew and be charged by Apple or Google. --- ## 19.
